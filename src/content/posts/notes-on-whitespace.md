title: Notes on whitespace
description: Empty space is not wasted space. A short case for letting a page breathe.
date: 2026-08-30
---

Whitespace is the quietest part of design and the part people notice most when it's missing.

## Measure

Keep lines around sixty to seventy characters. Eyes tire on long lines, and a narrow column makes even ordinary prose feel considered.

## Rhythm

Give headings room above them and less below. The page then reads in chunks, the way you'd speak: a pause, then the next thought.
