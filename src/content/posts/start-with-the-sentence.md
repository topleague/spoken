title: Start with the sentence

Most advice says to outline first. I've found the opposite works better for short pieces: write one sentence you actually believe, then find out what it needs.

## One true sentence

A true sentence is a small promise. It tells you what the next sentence owes the reader. If you can't write one, you don't have a post yet, you have a topic.

> A topic is where you start looking. A sentence is where you've already found something.

## Let the draft be ugly

The first pass is for you. Cut it down once you know what it was trying to say, and not before.
